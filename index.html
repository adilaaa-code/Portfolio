About Me

I’m Adila Ahmad Ramdani, a Multimedia Engineering Technology student at Politeknik Negeri Media Kreatif (GPA 3.77/4.00), specializing in graphic design, motion graphics, and social media content. I work with Adobe Illustrator, After Effects, Figma, and Canva to create visuals ranging from campaign posters and Instagram feeds to event videotron bumpers. I have led creative and publication teams for campus and regional events, delivering assets on schedule while keeping branding consistent. I’m a collaborative team player eager to grow in the creative industry.
